# Business Plan & Technical Setup: E-Commerce to WhatsApp Vape Shop

## 1. The Business Model
*   **Zero Inventory Dropshipping:** Sell products from the New Market store online without holding stock.
*   **Dual Revenue Stream:**
    1.  **Online Markup:** Add 20-50 BDT on top of the store's regular price.
    2.  **Commission:** 100 BDT per full kit, 50 BDT per cartridge/coil from the shop owner.
*   **Fulfillment:** Shop owner packs the nightly shipment; Pathao picks up and delivers.

## 2. Order Workflow (The Customer Journey)
1.  **Browse:** Customer visits the premium online store (optimized for mobile).
2.  **Cart:** Customer adds desired items to the cart (curated selection of 5-7 premium kits/juices).
3.  **Checkout:** Customer enters Name, Phone, and Delivery Address.
4.  **Payment Info:** Website shows the shop's bKash Personal Number and instructs the user to "Send Money".
5.  **WhatsApp Redirect:** Clicking "Confirm Order" generates a pre-filled WhatsApp message containing the order details, customer info, and total price.
6.  **Verification:** Customer sends the WhatsApp message along with the bKash screenshot/TrxID.
7.  **Fulfillment:** The order is verified, and the shop owner sends the package via Pathao.

## 3. UI/UX Design "Vibe"
*   **Theme:** Premium Dark Mode (Black/Dark Grey) with subtle Neon accents (Purple/Cyan).
*   **Simplicity:** Limited to 5-7 high-end, high-demand models to avoid choice paralysis and stock issues.
*   **Trust Signals:** "18+ Verification", "Verified New Market Seller", "Secure bKash Payments", "Fast Pathao Delivery".

## 4. Operational Strategy
*   **Order Routing (Highly Recommended):** WhatsApp orders should route to **your** number first. This allows you to verify the order, log your commission, and then forward the clean packing list to the shop owner. This ensures you know exactly how much commission you are owed at the end of the week.
*   **Stock Updates:** The owner just needs to let you know if one of these 5-7 core items is out of stock so you can temporarily hide it from the site.
